For contributors deciding whether source search is ready for a usefulness trial.

4/7 in the preceding revision and 1/7 in compact search.** The constructor and normal
Task entry are found, and all earlier macro/receiver/validator/rejection gains survive.
The efun table invocation remains missing. Keep selector defaults unchanged pending fresh
questions and a maintainer trial; this is known-source development evidence.

## What changed

The two candidate pools now have explicit Definition/Entry, Registration/Example or
Entry/Validation roles. Direct operation names rank before ownership. Only Definition
and Registration receive additional captured-ownership priority; other pools use it for
admission/context without promoting ordinary members ahead of external sites.

Direct name credit requires a callable function/method declaration. Container names and
ancestor words remain weak context. Raw macro kind supplies structural registration
evidence without expansion. Baseline anchors, exact source/range identities, same-name
diversity/backfill, literal bounds and stale-context validation remain unchanged.

A stronger fixture exposed that a container named Validation was still treated as a
validation method. The attempted survivor-run baseline failed and tested zero mutants.
Callable-only credit fixed that issue; focused tests passed and the complete final module
was mutation-tested anew. Preparatory and final evidence remain separate.
